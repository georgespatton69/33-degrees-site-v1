Shipping Policy

33 Degrees of Healing · Updated April 2026

Shipping Coverage

33 Degrees of Healing currently ships to addresses within the United States only. International shipping is not available at this time. As our operations expand, international options may be added in the future.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. Orders submitted on weekends or holidays will begin processing the next business day. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been submitted and a second notification when it ships.

Shipping Methods & Delivery Times

We offer two shipping options:

  • USPS Priority Mail — Estimated delivery in 2–3 business days
  • USPS First Class — Estimated delivery in 3–5 business days

Delivery times are estimates provided by USPS and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to carrier volume, weather, or other factors outside our control.

Free Shipping

Orders totaling $150 or more qualify for free standard shipping within the United States.

Order Tracking

A tracking number will be provided via email once your order has shipped. You can use this number to monitor your shipment's progress through the USPS tracking system.

Signature Requirements

Orders with a total value exceeding $200 may require a signature upon delivery. This measure helps ensure that high-value shipments are received securely.

Regulatory Compliance

All shipments are prepared and dispatched in compliance with applicable federal and state regulations governing the transport of research compounds.
